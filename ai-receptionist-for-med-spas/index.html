FAQ

Common questions from med spa owners evaluating AI receptionists.

Can an AI receptionist book med spa consultations?

Yes. MedspAI can follow your availability, service categories, and consult rules to place qualified callers into booking-ready next steps.

How does it handle pricing questions?

It provides approved pricing guidance and package context based on your rules, then escalates to staff when a nuanced quote is needed.

What happens if it can’t answer something?

It captures the caller’s intent and routes the conversation to your team with context so staff can continue without restarting discovery.

Can it transfer to my staff?

Yes. Transfer rules can be configured by call type, urgency, or time of day so the right person gets involved when required.

Does it work after hours?

Yes. After-hours coverage is a core use case, including intake capture, qualification, and missed-call text-back workflows.

How quickly can we get set up?

Most clinics can launch quickly once call-forwarding and intake rules are finalized during onboarding.

Will it fit our existing tools?

It is designed to fit into existing calendar, CRM, and phone-system workflows so your staff can keep familiar processes.
