Why Pricing Is Based on Call Volume

Clinics run on calls. As call demand increases, your receptionist configuration requires more capacity to maintain fast, consistent coverage and reliable routing.

This keeps pricing aligned with real demand: smaller clinics avoid over-committing, while growing clinics can scale without rebuilding core workflows.
